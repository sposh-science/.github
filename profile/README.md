## How SPOSH Began

SPOSH grew from our involvement with open tools for science and from the inspiration of the **Gathering for Open Science Hardware (GOSH)** community.

GOSH brought together a global community around the idea that scientific hardware should be open — enabling people to study, build, modify, reproduce and share the tools used to do science.

That global movement inspired a simple question:

> **Could we help build a similar community locally — bringing together people interested in Open Science Hardware, FOSS, scientific instrumentation and hands-on experimentation?**

That question became one of the ideas behind **SPOSH — Society for Promotion of Open Science Hardware**. We hope to become a small, local node in that wider GOSH movement — one small community that wishes to be part of it.

Our practical journey with Open Science Hardware was also shaped strongly by **ExpEYES — Experiments for Young Engineers and Scientists**.

Working with ExpEYES showed us firsthand that scientific instruments can be **open, affordable and understandable** — allowing students, educators and experimenters not only to take measurements, but also to understand and modify the instruments themselves.

That journey continues with **SEELab**, extending open experimentation, measurement and data acquisition, and **KuttyPy**, bringing together programming, electronics and physical computing.

ExpEYES, SEELab and KuttyPy, developed by **CSpark Research**, have been an important part of our experience with open tools for science, together with the wider open-source community that has contributed to and grown around them.

SPOSH brings these experiences together: inspiration from a **global Open Science Hardware community**, hands-on experience with **open scientific tools**, and a desire to create a **local community where people can meet, learn, build and collaborate**.

> **Think globally. Build locally. Share openly.**

## About SPOSH

**SPOSH — Society for Promotion of Open Science Hardware** is a community that is still taking shape.

It grew from a simple belief: scientific tools should be **open, accessible, understandable and reproducible**, and people should be able to study, modify and improve the tools they use for science.

Our interests bring together **Open Science Hardware, FOSS, scientific instrumentation, electronics, research tools and hands-on experimentation**.

Right now, our main focus is simple:

> **Find like-minded people. Connect. Build together. Grow the community.**

Researchers, educators, students, engineers, developers, makers and curious minds are all welcome.

---

## What We Do

- **Build & share** open scientific hardware and software
- **Explore & promote** open tools for science, research and education
- **Document & validate** what we build and experiment with
- **Connect people** interested in Open Science Hardware and FOSS

> **Documentation is part of the instrument — not an afterthought.**

---

## What We Believe

**Open by Design · Reproducible · Transparent · Collaborative · Accessible**

Scientific tools should not have to be black boxes.

We want to understand how they work, document what we learn, test them carefully and make it easier for others to **build, reproduce, modify and improve** them.

Open Science Hardware is not only about making scientific tools less expensive. It is also about making science more **transparent, adaptable, collaborative and reproducible**.
