## Our Open Hardware Journey

Our journey into Open Science Hardware began with **ExpEYES — Experiments for Young Engineers and Scientists**.

ExpEYES introduced us to an important idea: scientific instruments can be **open, affordable and understandable** — allowing students, educators and experimenters not only to take measurements, but also to understand the tools behind them.

That journey continues with **SEELab**, extending the possibilities of open experimentation, measurement and data acquisition, and with **KuttyPy**, bringing together programming, electronics and physical computing.

Developed and supported by **CSpark Research** and their open-source communities, these platforms have played an important role in shaping our interest in open tools for science.

We continue to use, explore and promote them — from the classroom and makerspace to **experimentation, instrumentation and research**.

In many ways, SPOSH grew from this journey:

> **Use open tools. Understand them. Experiment with them. Improve them. Build new things together.**

## About SPOSH

**SPOSH — Society for Promotion of Open Science Hardware** is a community that is still taking shape.

It grew from a simple belief: scientific tools should be **open, accessible, understandable and reproducible**, and people should be able to study, modify and improve the tools they use for science.

Our interests bring together **Open Science Hardware, FOSS, scientific instrumentation, electronics, research tools and hands-on experimentation**.

Right now, our main focus is simple:

> **Find like-minded people. Connect. Build together. Grow the community.**

Researchers, educators, students, engineers, developers, makers and curious minds are all welcome.

## What We Do

- **Build & share** open scientific hardware and software
- **Explore & promote** open tools for science, research and education
- **Document & validate** what we build and experiment with
- **Connect people** interested in Open Science Hardware and FOSS

> **Documentation is part of the instrument — not an afterthought.**

---

## What We Believe

**Open by Design · Reproducible · Transparent · Collaborative · Accessible**

Scientific tools should not have to be black boxes.

We want to understand how they work, document what we learn, test them carefully and make it easier for others to **build, reproduce, modify and improve** them.

Open Science Hardware is not only about making scientific tools less expensive. It is also about making science more **transparent, adaptable, collaborative and reproducible**.
